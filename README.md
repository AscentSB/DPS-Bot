# DPS Bot

Discord bot for Hypixel SkyBlock dungeons. `/dps` reads your gear and lists your next upgrades, ranked by weighted % DPS gained per million coins.

## Command

`/dps archer|berserker ign:<name> [target]` and `/dps mage ign:<name> [set:<Boss set (LCM)|Clear set (RCM)>] [floor:<Master Mode|Normal Mode>] [target]`. The reply shows 8 upgrades per page (40 planned) with Back / Next buttons, Coin upgrades / Profile upgrades buttons to switch lists, and a button to switch between every level (Duplex I, II, ...) and max levels only. Only the person who ran the command can press them; buttons expire after 30 minutes. Each user can run /dps once every 10 seconds.

`upgrades` picks how leveled upgrades are listed. All levels (default) lists Duplex I, II, III... as separate steps. Max level only jumps straight to Duplex V at the price of the Duplex V book, all remaining master stars in one step, Perfect gems, and max essence perk levels.

1. Looks up your UUID at `https://api.mojang.com/users/profiles/minecraft/<name>`.
2. Pulls your selected SkyBlock profile from Hypixel. It reads every item by `ITEM_ID` (inventory, armor, equipment, wardrobe, ender chest, backpacks, vault, accessory bag), your Catacombs level, secrets, pets, selected power and tuning.
3. Picks your best owned item for each slot.
4. Tries every upgrade: master stars, enchant books, potato books, The Art of War, recombobulators, reforge stones, blacksmith reforges (Blended on equipment, Light on boots), gemstones (with slot unlock costs), fragging (8 boss fragments), enrichments, attribute shards, essence shop perks, pets and pet items, ability items, and buying any weapon, armor, equipment, accessory or Ragnarock you don't own. Gear is also offered bundled with its best reforge stone, so a piece that is only worth it reforged (Necron's armor with Ancient) still shows up.
5. Takes the best % per million, applies it, recalculates every option from the new stats, and repeats. A master star never lands on an item you don't have, and the 5th never comes before the 4th. Nothing locks: Duplex I can be followed by Duplex II and later V, and a later reforge or purchase can replace an earlier one, as long as each step adds damage. If the plan later replaces a piece of armor or equipment, it spends under 25M on that piece first (buy plus upgrades): the plan is rebuilt with that budget until no replaced piece goes over (`REPLACED_PIECE_BUDGET`). Upgrades that keep everything on the item do not count as a replacement: Golden boss heads into Diamond heads (8 Enchanted Diamond Blocks), Wither armor into Necron's / Storm's / Maxor's / Goldor's (8 Giant fragments), and fragging. The planner offers these as their own steps (`src/config/itemUpgrades.js`) and never offers a fresh copy of what a worn item upgrades into. A step that a later step overwrites (a reforge, enchant level, gem, enrichment, pet item, piece or pet) with 10M coins or less spent in between is skipped unless it alone adds more than 5% damage (`REPLACED_SOON_COINS`, `REPLACED_SOON_MIN_GAIN`).

Only gear the player can use is offered: Catacombs level, floor completion (from Catacombs level), skill and slayer requirements from the items resource are checked, and shards need the Hunting level to syphon them (Uncommon 5, Rare 10, Epic 15, Legendary 20).

Prices come from bazaar instant buy and auction house lowest BIN. NPC prices are never used. Essence shop perks cost essence × the bazaar price of that essence, and only show at Catacombs 20+ (the bazaar requirement for essence). Blacksmith reforges roll at random for 500 coins, so they cost 500 × the number of basic reforges for that item type (9 for equipment, so 4,500 coins).

## Weights

| Class | Wither | Dragon |
|---|---|---|
| Archer | 50 | 50 |
| Mage | 80 | 20 |
| Berserker | 20 | 80 |

An upgrade's gain is the weighted average of its % gain against each boss: one that only helps against dragons counts 80% for Berserker, 50% for Archer and 20% for Mage (the reverse for withers only), one that helps both equally counts 100%, and one that helps them by different amounts is weighted by the table. Picking `target` puts all 100 on that boss. The Mage clear set (RCM) and Normal Mode count withers / clear mobs only.

## Model

- Blessings: Power 19 (strength, crit damage), Wisdom 12 (intelligence) and Time 5 (strength, intelligence; from the Quiz, set it to 0 or 5). Each adds `4 × level × E` flat and multiplies by `1 + 0.02 × level × E`, where `E = 1.2 floor buff × (1 + Forbidden Blessing) × (1 + Mimic shard)`. With Forbidden Blessing X and Mimic X, E is the original 1.1 × 1.1 × 1.2.
- Strength: `(flat + blessing flat)(1 + additive + 2nd additive)(multiplicative)(blessing %)`. Crit damage and intelligence use the same shape.
- Pets: one per boss. Golden Dragon against withers for every class. Ender Dragon against dragons for Archer and Berserker (dragons are Ender mobs), Golden Dragon for Mage (Chimera copies its attack speed). Without that pet, the owned pet that does the most damage against that boss is used (`PHASE_PETS` in `src/config/pets.js`). All pets count as level 100 (Golden Dragon 200) and are priced from max-level auction listings. Base stats come from NEU; Pet perks modeled: Golden Dragon, Ender Dragon, Jellyfish, Wither Skeleton (Wither Blood +100% vs withers, Death's Touch as an extra 67% hit every second), Skeleton (Bone Arrows doubled in dungeons, Combo +60 strength with a bow), Blaze (weapon potato books count twice), Pigman (+75% vs level 100+), Tiger (Apex Predator vs withers only), T-Rex (Tyrant boosts pet items), Lion (Primal Force weapon stats, First Pounce on First Strike / Triple-Strike), Griffin (+15% strength), Ankylosaurus (+500 strength cap) and Black Cat (+100 speed and speed cap). Never used: Horse and Skeleton Horse (Jump Boost). Not counted by choice: Zombie, Megalodon, Giraffe, Golem, Snowman, Guardian, Parrot, Bee (no teammates), Bingo, Bat, Flying Fish, Rock, Web Battlefield, Enderman. Ender Dragon's Superior perk (+10%) and Legion also raise attack speed, tuning points included.
- Tier Boost: a pet with a Tier Boost plays one rarity higher (an Epic Ender Dragon plays as Legendary). Pets can be bought a rarity lower plus a Tier Boost. Swapping the Tier Boost for another pet item can come with a Kat upgrade to keep the rarity: Kat's level-100 cost (30% off), its items, one Kat Flower and the new item.
- Dungeon VII potion: Strength V, Critical III, Archery III (+50% bow damage) and Speed III, always on. A Jellyfish makes it up to 50% stronger and keeps working after you swap pets.
- Armor: one piece per slot. Necron's armor and Necron heads are Archer and Berserker gear only; Mage tops out at Storm's armor. Set and piece bonuses (Skeleton Master, Maxor's, Superior Dragon, Young Dragon, Adaptive) are in `src/config/armorSets.js`. Necron heads give 2x stats on Floor VII.
- DPS = damage per arrow × arrows per volley × volleys per second. Terminator fires 3 arrows + 0.04 per Duplex level + Archer Doubleshot (100% as the only Archer + 1% per Archer level, so 1.5 at level 50), every ceil(10 / (1 + attack speed/100)) ticks, so 4 volleys/s at 100 attack speed. Berserkers use the Terminator too.
- Weird and Weirder Tuba: Howl gets used during dragons only, so the buff (Weirder: +40 strength, +5 attack speed, +10 crit damage) counts against dragons only. Attack speed and volleys are worked out per target.
- Armor only ever gets Ancient (`ARMOR_REFORGE`). Other armor reforges are never offered or assumed.
- Tuning: one setup for the whole run. Leftover points use the best split of strength, crit damage and crit chance for your loadout (10% steps), not the split saved on the profile, and the embed shows it. Points fill attack speed to 100 for whichever boss needs more (usually dragons, with Ender Dragon) first (0.3 per point), then speed to 500 (1.5 per point); only the rest go to crit chance / crit damage. A Crochet Tiger or Hephaestus Plushie on your pet is the usual way to free the attack speed points.
- Speed counts base 100, the Dungeon VII potion, Wolf Slayer, the Rift Blessing of Time perk, gear, reforges, Sugar Rush, accessories, enrichments, Obsidian Defender and Forbidden Speed. Speed only matters in the clear, so your fastest boots (e.g. Maxor's) count for speed while your boss boots count for damage. With Maxor's boots, Archer and Berserker boss boots stay Maxor's unless you swap to Necron's. Mage clears in RCM armor and is scored in the boss only: no clear boots, any boss boots (Storm's beat Maxor's), and tuning fills speed to 450 instead of 500. The Rogue Sword is not counted; `EXTRA_SPEED` covers anything else.
- Beacon: a Tier V Beacon powered by Scorched Power Crystals (+20%) is assumed on crit chance (+6) for Archer and Berserker and on ability damage (+6%) for Mage (no effect on the LCM beam; it is for RCM).
- Crit chance: `(flat + 30 + 0.5 × Combat level)(1 + additive)(multiplicative)`. Terminator divides it by 4 (0.25). Combat is read from the profile, maxed (60) when the skills API is off.
- Archer and Berserker hits use the expected value of crits below 100% crit chance and overload between 100% and 200%.
- Class bonuses (each class assumed solo, which doubles its class bonuses): Archer gets x(1 + 1.6% per Archer level) arrow damage, multiplicative (x1.8 at 50). Berserker gets 40 + 1.5 per Berserk level additive damage (115 at 50). Archer and Berserker also get Combat's Warrior bonus: +4 additive per level to 50, +1 per level after (210 at Combat 60).
- Dungeon gear (every dungeon item assumed 5-starred, dungeonizable items like Terminator assumed converted): `stat × (cata boost + 1.5 + 0.05 × master stars + 0.01 × secret digits)`. Crit chance and attack speed skip the cata boost. Speed gets the full multiplier, so Maxor's boots (30 speed) give 30 × 6.35 = 190.5 at Catacombs 50 before master stars and the medallion. Secret digits need a General's Medallion.
- Floor-scaled dungeon armor (Skeleton Master and other pieces with per-floor stats) is assumed dropped from the highest floor your Catacombs level allows (floor 10 at 36, 9 at 34, 8 at 32, 7 at 24, 6 at 19, 5 at 14, 4 at 9, 3 at 5, 2 at 3, 1 at 1) with the full 50% stat boost, which also raises it one rarity.
- Dungeon accessories and their enrichments get x (1 + cata boost) on every stat except crit chance and attack speed (no stars or medallion on accessories). Every damage enrichment is offered: crit damage, crit chance, strength, attack speed and speed.
- A buy can come bundled with its recombobulator, enrichment or reforge as one step, with the prices and gains combined before ranking.
- Accessory Power: `29.97 × ln(0.0019 × AP + 1)^1.2` times your power's stats, plus tuning points and enrichments. Dungeon accessories count double.
- Mage is scored as LCM (the mage beam, used in the boss). Mage Staff: each beam deals (0.3 + 0.0009 × intelligence) of the melee hit (strength, crit damage, crit chance, overload), ×1.5 vs withers while holding Necron's Blade or a refinement (Hyperion, Astraea, Valkyrie, Scylla; any class, only the held weapon). Ability damage does not boost the beam (that is RCM). Beams per second = 20 / round(10 / (1 + attack speed/100)), so attack speed matters. Mage class gives 250 + 5 intelligence per Mage level, doubled per level when solo (750 at 50). Chimera on the Mage weapon copies the active pet's stats, which then get the weapon's dungeon boost. Tuning splits leftover points between strength, crit damage, crit chance and intelligence.
- Mage boss set (LCM, Master Mode) can swap gear for dragons: a dragon set holds a second weapon, armor pieces or equipment that replace the main set against dragons only (e.g. Hyperion for withers, Dark Claymore for dragons). Each set gets its own tuning. Owned gear is sorted into both sets, and a buy can go into the main set, the dragon set, or replace the main piece for withers while the old one stays for dragons. Upgrades on a dragon-set piece only count toward dragon damage. Mage weapon buys can also come with the current weapon's enchants (books up to 10M, or all enchants plus master stars) and potato books, priced as books, so a new blade is not compared bare against a maxed one.
- Mage clear set (`mage:Clear set (RCM)`): scored on one Wither Impact cast against a clear mob, on a Hyperion, Astraea, Valkyrie or Scylla (no blade = 0). `10,000 × (1 + cata boost) × (1 + intelligence / 100 × 0.3) × (1 + ability damage / 100) × (1 + magic additive / 100) × Loving 1.05`, then the soft cap. Magic additive is pet perks (Golden Dragon's Legendary Treasure), Combat's Warrior and weapon damage enchants that do not say melee and hit clear mobs (Smite yes; Sharpness is melee only, Ender Slayer is Ender mobs only). Strength, crits and Fabled do not count. Ability damage: gear (stars only), Beacon, Mage class (+5, +1 per 5 levels, doubled solo), Enchanting (+0.5% per level), and Chimera copying a Sheep. Boss-only bonuses (vs withers or dragons) do not apply. Tuning fills speed to 500 (the whole set is worn in the clear), then intelligence. Any armor reforge is allowed (Necrotic, Loving). The best clear pet is picked freely. Minibosses can be LCM'd with the boss set, so the clear set ignores LCM.
- Sword enchants: Sharpness, Critical, Smite (withers), Ender Slayer (dragons), Giant Killer (bosses and clear mobs out-HP you, so it sits at its cap), Prosecute (counted at full target health: early damage matters most since Lethality speeds up the rest, and RCM one-taps), Thunderlord (every 3rd hit adds a lightning hit of 8-60% of the hit, soft-capped on its own), Swarm (+2-10% per enemy within 10 blocks: 1 in the boss, 7 in the clear) and Chimera. Master Mode counts Titan Killer (cap, high-defense mobs) and Triple-Strike (M7). The `floor` toggle is for the Mage boss set (LCM) only: Normal Mode (F7) counts First Strike instead, drops Titan Killer, master stars and the Master Skull (Master Mode only), and puts all the weight on withers (F7 has no dragons). Archer, Berserker and the RCM clear set are always Master Mode. Execute (missing health), Thunderbolt (worse than Thunderlord on one target) and Cubism (no creepers or cubes) are not counted. Melee-only enchants (Sharpness, First Strike, Triple-Strike, Thunderlord) do not boost RCM.
- RCM Chimera: Chimera V + a Crow or Sheep with a Hephaestus Relic (+50% to every pet stat, ability damage included) is offered as one step, since Chimera alone copies nothing useful from Golden Dragon in the clear.
- Necron's Blade refinements get their per-Catacombs-level stats as base stats (dungeon scaled with the rest): Hyperion +1 damage and +2 intelligence per level, Valkyrie +1 damage and strength, Scylla +1 damage and crit damage, Astraea +1 damage.
- Wither scrolls (Implosion, Shadow Warp, Wither Shield) are read from the item. All 3 (or the Ultimate Wither Scroll) give Wither Impact; 2 of 3 does nothing. RCM needs them on the blade you cast with (a bought blade comes with them). In LCM, a Shadow Assassin Cloak with any owned Wither Impact blade gives 1.1x (every hit follows a teleport). Missing scrolls are priced as one step.
- Powers: the best learned power is used (switching at Maxwell is free) and re-picked after every step. Unlearned powers are a step priced at their power stone. One Accessory Enrichment Swapper changes every enriched accessory left in the bag to the enrichment you pick, so swaps are one step for one swapper: all enriched accessories, only the dungeon ones or only the regular ones (take the others out first).
- Profile stats (when the profile is read): base 50 crit damage, Foraging strength, Enchanting and Alchemy intelligence (+1 per level to 14, +2 after), Tarantula, Wolf and Enderman slayer rewards, +1 strength per 5 SkyBlock levels, the Book of Progression (in the bag and set to Extra Infliction, read from the profile; SkyBlock level 75+: x(1 + 0.01% per SkyBlock level), capped at 500. If it is set to something else, switching is a step costing 500 coins x SkyBlock level), Melody's Harp songs played perfectly (up to +26 intelligence) and active Century Cake buffs. Fairy souls and the bestiary give no damage stats. Mayor perks are never counted.
- The Profile upgrades button lists the next level of each profile source (Catacombs, your class, Combat, Foraging, Enchanting, Alchemy, slayers, the next 5 SkyBlock levels, each Harp song) ranked by % damage, with no coin cost. Coin upgrades (default) is the buy list.
- Below floor 10 (Catacombs under 36), floor-scaled dungeon armor gets no step over 1M coins (it is replaced at floor 10).
- Ferocity: Berserker against withers only (not dragons, never Archer or Mage). Each 100 ferocity is one extra hit per arrow, a melee hit, so it takes the normal hit (additive included) times the Berserk melee bonus as a multiplier (1 + (40 + 1.5 per level) / 100), soft-capped on its own, then halved: ferocity hits land about 0.5s late and Goldor, the only place Berserker procs it, lives about 1s (`FEROCITY_EFFECTIVENESS`). Ferocity on dungeon gear gets stars and the medallion only. Sources: gear, Vicious (+3/+4/+5 at III-V, Terminator included), reforges (Dirty), pets (Tiger, Hound, ...) and Tiger's Merciless Swipe.
- Arrows: Archer always uses Nansorb Arrows and Berserker Bouncy Arrows: +5 bow damage each (their piercing / bouncing does nothing against one boss).
- Fabled reforge: every melee hit deals 0-15% more, multiplicative, counted as 1.075x (LCM beam and sword hits, not RCM).
- All damage above 1,000,000 keeps 10%.

The hit formula reproduces the M7 archer sheet's output exactly, and the blessing math matches the wiki's worked example (see `test/formulas.test.js`).

## Setup

```
npm install
cp .env.example .env   # fill in DISCORD_TOKEN, CLIENT_ID, HYPIXEL_API_KEY, optional GUILD_ID
npm run deploy         # register /dps (run again whenever the command's options change)
npm start
```

- Node 20.10 or newer. The bot needs to stay running (a VPS, a home server or any Node host).
- `DISCORD_TOKEN` and `CLIENT_ID` come from the Discord Developer Portal (Bot page and General Information). Invite the bot with the `applications.commands` and `bot` scopes. Without `GUILD_ID` the command registers globally and can take up to an hour to appear.
- `HYPIXEL_API_KEY` reads profiles. Each player's profile is cached for 5 minutes, so repeat runs in that window show the same data and use no key requests. A development key works for testing; a bot others use needs a production key from https://developer.hypixel.net. Bazaar and auction prices need no key.
- Threads (`src/worker`): the planner runs in a plan worker, so long plans never block the Discord connection. Prices download and decode in a separate price worker (one refresh per source at a time; auction pages 6 at a time with retries, and a page that still fails keeps the last full auction house). Each refresh first reads page 0 and skips the rest when Hypixel's `lastUpdated` has not changed. Auction prices are the median lowest BIN over the last 3 changed downloads (only for items still listed), so one sniped or bought-out listing does not swing the plan. Candidate scoring spreads over an evaluation pool (up to 4 workers, `EVAL_WORKERS` to change or 0 to turn off) with the exact same results as one thread.
- Planner speed: item, accessory, shard and pet stats are cached per (immutable) object; re-plans resume from the first step the new rules reject; every distinct loadout's scored candidates are cached by content.

## Where to edit

- `src/config/itemStats.js`: item ids and the stats they grant, misc buffs, reforge overrides.
- `src/config/pets.js`: pet items and pet perks.
- `src/config/armorSets.js`: armor set and piece bonuses.
- `src/config/upgrades.js`: ability items.
- `src/config/essence.js`: essence shop perks.
- `src/config/shards.js`: attribute shards.
- `src/config/enchants.js`: enchant stats per level and which boss they affect.
- `src/config/accessories.js`: Accessory Power, powers, tuning, enrichments.
- `src/config/gemstones.js`: gemstone stats.
- `src/config/constants.js`: weights, boss defense, blessing levels (Time 0 or 5; mayor perks are never counted), Catacombs table, soft cap.
- `src/calc/formulas.js`: the damage and stat formulas.

`npm run update-data` refreshes reforge stats, accessory families, fragged item recipes and pet stats from the NotEnoughUpdates repo. `npm test` runs the tests.
